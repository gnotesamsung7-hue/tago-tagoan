How to play

👀 You see everything through your own eyes. Your flashlight only reaches a few steps, so listen carefully — monster footsteps and kids’ giggles come from the left or right speaker. Headphones help!

🧟 If a monster sees you, it chases you. Run, turn corners, and break its line of sight. Glowing red eyes in the dark mean one is watching.

📢 Watch the noise meter. Running is loud, walking is quieter, standing still is silent.

🫣 Walk up to a bed, closet, sofa, drum or tree and press Hide. You’ll peek out from inside. If a monster saw you hide, press Hold breath until it leaves.

🔎 In Hanap! mode, search hiding spots to find the 5 kids. Follow the giggles.

🗺 The little map shows where you are. Monsters show up on it only when they’re close.

Phone: drag the left side to walk, swipe the right side to look. Keyboard: WS walk, AD step sideways, ←→ or mouse to look, Q turn around, Shift run, E hide, F search, Space hold breath, P pause.
