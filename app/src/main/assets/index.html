How to play

👀 You see everything through your own eyes. Your flashlight only reaches a few steps, so listen — monster footsteps and kids’ giggles come from the left or right speaker. Headphones help!

🧟 If a monster sees you, it chases you. Run, turn corners, and break its line of sight. Red eyes in the dark mean one is watching.

📢 Watch the noise meter. Running is loud, walking is quieter, standing still is silent.

🫣 Walk up to a bed, closet, drum, tomb or tree and press Hide. If a monster saw you hide, press Hold breath until it leaves.

🔎 In Hanap!, search hiding spots to find the 5 kids. Follow the giggles.

🗺 Four stages: Bahay ni Lola, Paaralan, Palengke and Sementeryo.

📶 Wi-Fi games (Android app): hide together from the monsters, hunt the kids as a team, or play Taya — one player is the monster wearing their own face. The Taya counts to 10 first, then hunts. Taya sees 🔊 when a hider is loud and can search hiding spots. Caught hiders become ghosts 👻 and can keep watching.

Phone: drag the left side to walk, swipe the right side to look. Keyboard: WS walk, AD step sideways, ←→ or mouse to look, Q turn around, Shift run, E hide, F search, Space hold breath, P pause.
